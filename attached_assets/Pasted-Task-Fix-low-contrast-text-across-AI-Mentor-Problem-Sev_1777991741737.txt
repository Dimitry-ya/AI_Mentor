==================================================
CONTRAST RULES
==================================================

Use these contrast targets:

1. Normal body text:
- must meet at least WCAG AA 4.5:1 contrast ratio against its background.

2. Large/bold text:
- must meet at least 3:1 contrast ratio.

3. Non-text UI indicators/icons:
- should meet at least 3:1 where they communicate meaning.

4. Disabled text:
- can be lower contrast, but must be clearly disabled and not used for normal readable content.

5. Placeholder/helper text:
- may be softer than primary text, but must still be readable.
- Do not use placeholder-level contrast for labels or normal content.

==================================================
TEXT HIERARCHY
==================================================

Normalize text hierarchy:

1. Primary text:
Use for important values, titles, user-readable content.
Must be high contrast.

2. Secondary text:
Use for labels, metadata, descriptions.
Must still be readable.
Do not make it too pale.

3. Tertiary text:
Use only for low-priority hints.
Still should be legible.

4. Disabled text:
Use only for disabled/unavailable states.

Important:
Do not use disabled-looking gray for normal labels.
In the current UI, many normal labels look disabled. Fix that.
